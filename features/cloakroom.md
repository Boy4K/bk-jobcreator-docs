# Cloakroom

At the cloakroom point:

* **Wear your grade's outfit** – the uniform saved for your grade.
* **Save a new outfit** / **Delete an outfit** – for the boss, per grade.
* **Personal outfits** – opens the outfits saved by the player, with illenium-appearance or fivem-appearance.

Outfits are saved by the resource itself: no clothing resource is required.
