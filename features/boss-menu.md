# Boss menu

Opened at the job's boss menu point, by the boss.

<figure><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Boy4K/bk-jobcreator-docs/main/assets/boss-menu.jpg" alt="Dashboard"><figcaption><p>Dashboard</p></figcaption></figure>

* **Dashboard:** company funds, clean and dirty money, deposit and withdraw, taxes set aside.
* **Employees:** online and offline, play time, hire date, notes. Hire the player nearby, promote, demote, fire.
* **Grades:** list of grades and salaries.
* **Invoices:** open and paid, with search.
