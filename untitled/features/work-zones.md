# Work zones

An area you draw on the ground. It can pay a wage for the time spent inside, spawn litter to clean, or both.

## Drawing a zone

**New zone**, then **Walk the outline**: walk around the area and press `E` at each corner, `ENTER` when done. You can limit the zone in height (one floor only).

## Paid shift

* Minutes between wages, paid to bank or cash.
* Wage per grade (a grade without its own entry uses the closest one below).
* Taken from the company account or not; pay the boss too or not.
* Leaving the zone pauses the timer.

## Litter

Litter appears on its own at the spots you place, up to a maximum at once. Cleaning a piece gives an item and XP.

## The HUD

Time to the next wage, the wage, colleagues in the zone, level and XP. The boss sees who is in the zone.
