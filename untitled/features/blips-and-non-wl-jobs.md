# Blips and non-WL jobs

## Blips

The **Blips** tab manages map blips: name, sprite, scale, colour (picked from a grid of the real game colours) and position (taken where you stand).

## Jobs without whitelist

The **Non-WL jobs** tab creates simple open jobs (lumberjack, fisherman, miner...) with three points: **gathering**, **processing** and **selling**.
