# Duty menu (F6)

`F6` (or `/jobmenu`) opens the job menu. Players can change the key in GTA settings.

* **Go off duty:** you stop working; your post stays yours. Off duty you get no wage and the job points don't work for you.
* **Go on duty:** back to your job and grade.

Enable it per job with **Duty** in the job editor. With **Law enforcement** on, colleagues get a radio notification with sound when someone goes on or off duty, and everyone starts off duty at login.
